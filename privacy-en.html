About this policy

This policy covers Portraits of Being II, a standalone VR music and art experience for Meta Quest, including its on-device interaction and any support correspondence with the publisher.

Head, hand and posture information

The app receives headset position and orientation, abstracted hand-joint information and pinch states from the Quest/OpenXR tracking system. It uses these on the device for the VR viewpoint, spatial sound, the visible avatar, the start gesture and hand-controlled visual and sound trails. Short, bounded visual histories allow trails to remain briefly after movement.

The app estimates the current visitor's posture and eye-height reference to adapt the avatar. This is not eye tracking, identity recognition or a service that measures the whole body. The visitor reference is reset for a new visitor session. These values are processed in runtime memory; the release does not continuously save head or hand coordinates to app diagnostic files. Temporary state is cleared by the relevant session/reset lifecycle or when the app process ends.

The app does not record microphone audio or raw camera video. Using hand tracking does not mean the app receives raw tracking-camera images. Speech and music in the work are prerecorded media, not recordings of the visitor. The app does not provide a live text-to-speech service or send visitor input to ElevenLabs.

Meta account age category

For its Mixed Ages release, the app uses the official Meta Platform service to request the current Meta account's broad age category: child, teenager, adult or unknown. When online, it tries asynchronously to request this category for each new foreground user session, including returning to the foreground after a pause. This is an account-provided category, not an estimate of the person wearing the headset based on body height. No date of birth or exact age is requested from the visitor.

The Meta SDK and platform services associate initialization and the age request with the currently signed-in platform account and process the necessary platform identifiers. The app's age response contains only the broad category. The app does not separately fetch a user profile, name, profile photo, access token or raw user ID, and does not save raw platform IDs in its settings or ordinary logs.

The category is kept only in session memory and is cleared when the app leaves the foreground, goes offline or ends. It is not written to artist settings, a cloud save or a publisher database; the app's session handler does not include the category value in its own diagnostic messages. The result supports age-appropriate platform and data handling. The work provides no advertising, user chat, social or multiplayer features for any age. Offline, failed, timed-out and unknown results do not block preparation, gesture start or playback. When connectivity returns, the app can retry the request without restarting the artwork.

Local settings, cloud saves and removal

Saved presentation, audio, visual quality and gesture settings can remain in this app's local storage. An optional exhibition-area setting can retain an area mode and seat height; it is not a history of individual visitors' body measurements. A later launch may read these settings. Starting a new visitor session does not erase every saved setting.

The app does not provide app-data cloud saves. Android app-data backup is explicitly disabled in this release, and participation in Meta Automatic Cloud Backup is switched off. These choices preserve normal local settings and do not themselves perform a backup, restore, cloud-data deletion or local-data clear.

Cloud backup and device-to-device migration are different. On Android 12 and later, some device migration behavior can depend on the device manufacturer. These settings are not a guarantee against every migration method or a statement that all earlier platform data has been erased.

To remove this app's local settings, clear its app storage through the device's app-management options where available, or uninstall this specific app. This also removes saved presentation choices. A new visitor session resets the temporary visitor reference without requiring deletion of artist settings. Device/platform records and support messages are handled separately.

Connectivity, diagnostics and platform services

The artwork runs without a Mac, Max/MSP or separate app registration. The age check uses the Meta account already signed in on the device; it does not add an app login or a requirement to remain online. The release disables the developer's legacy OSC connection and continuous diagnostic-file capture. It does not implement advertising, social accounts, in-app purchases or a developer service that receives continuous head/hand recordings. The official age request is separate from those disabled developer export paths.

Unity cloud analytics, advertising and cloud crash/performance reporting are configured off, no Unity Cloud project is connected, and the effective engine diagnostic setting was confirmed disabled. Unity/OpenXR components include conditional technical initialization and error-reporting paths; initialization information can describe runtime and SDK versions and enabled features rather than head/hand coordinates. The Meta SDK and platform services may also process technical initialization, service responses and error information needed to provide account-linked platform functions. These provider-controlled paths are separate from the app session handler's own messages and from the Unity diagnostic setting. Disabled developer export paths are not a promise that every native SDK, device or operating-system service makes no connection. The device can still generate essential error messages and process platform information under its own controls.

For provider information, see the Supplemental Meta Platforms Technologies Privacy Policy, Meta Hand Tracking Privacy Notice and Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy. These explain the providers' services and do not replace this app's policy.

Younger users and changes

The publisher intends the work for a general audience without an additional app-specific age restriction. Use must still comply with Meta Quest's minimum-age, account and parental requirements. The local tracking and limited platform age-category handling above also apply to younger users; the app does not create persistent child profiles, ask for dates of birth or provide user chat. A parent or guardian may contact the publisher about support information provided by a child.

The publisher will update this policy when relevant processing changes and keep the applicable policy versions. Questions or requests about publisher-held information can be sent to the privacy contact above.
